## YEAR 3: VERTICAL SPECIALIZATION & INTERNATIONAL (2028)

### Vision: Vertical-Specific Solutions Generate 40%+ of Revenue

**Goal:** Move beyond horizontal "one-size-fits-all" to vertical-specific platforms. Expand internationally. Approach $100M ARR.

**Key milestones:**
- Q2 2028: Series C closing ($100M+)
- Vertical solutions launched (Retail, Agencies, Healthcare)
- International expansion (10+ countries)
- $100M ARR, 80,000 customers, 70-person team

### Product Strategy: Vertical Specialization

**Why verticals:**
- Horizontal all-in-one can't serve all verticals equally well
- Verticals have specific workflows and features (e.g., retail = inventory, healthcare = patient records)
- Pricing power (vertical solutions can command premium pricing)
- Network effects (specialized features attract specialists, feedback improves product)

**Vertical 1: Retail & E-Commerce (30% of business by 2028)**
- POS integration, inventory management, customer loyalty
- Targeting: 5,000+ SMB retailers
- Pricing: $199-499/month (premium for retail-specific features)
- Revenue potential: $15M+ ARR

**Vertical 2: Agencies & Services (25% of business by 2028)**
- Project management, time tracking, resource allocation
- Targeting: 5,000+ agencies
- Pricing: $149-299/month
- Revenue potential: $12M+ ARR

**Vertical 3: Healthcare (20% of business by 2028)**
- Patient records, appointment scheduling, HIPAA compliance
- Targeting: 5,000+ healthcare practices
- Pricing: $199-399/month
- Revenue potential: $10M+ ARR

**Vertical 4: SaaS/Tech (15% of business by 2028)**
- Developer tools, API management, advanced automation
- Targeting: 3,000+ SaaS companies
- Pricing: $299-599/month
- Revenue potential: $8M+ ARR

**Horizontal Core (10% of business by 2028)**
- Original all-in-one CRM for general SMBs
- Maintains $10M+ ARR from breadth

**Platform leverage:**
- All verticals share core CRM, dashboard, automation engine
- Vertical-specific modules layer on top
- Single codebase with modular architecture
- **Impact:** Scalable vertical expansion (each new vertical leverages platform)

### International Expansion

**Phase 1 (2028): UK, Canada, Australia (English-speaking)**
- Launch with localized support (customer success team)
- Partner with local agencies and consultants
- Target: 20%+ of customers from ANZ/UK/Canada

**Phase 2 (2028-2029): Europe (Germany, France, Netherlands)**
- GDPR-compliant infrastructure (multi-region deployment)
- Local language support (German, French)
- Partner with European consultants and integrators
- Target: 15%+ of customers from Western Europe

**Phase 3 (2029+): Asia-Pacific (Singapore, Japan, Australia)**
- Singapore as APAC hub
- Japanese market entry (tech-forward SMBs)
- Australia expansion (already English-speaking)
- Target: 10%+ of customers from APAC

### Financial Targets

- **Revenue**: $25M → $100M ARR (4x growth)
- **Customers**: 30,000 → 80,000 (2.67x growth, higher ACV from verticals)
- **ARPU**: $110 → $165 (vertical-specific premium pricing)
- **CAC**: $380 → $350 (vertical targeting improves efficiency)
- **LTV:CAC**: 15:1 → 25:1 (exceptional)
- **Operating margin**: 60% → 65% (scale advantages)
- **Cash generation**: +$5M/month positive cash flow
- **Valuation**: $500M-1B (10x revenue multiple typical)

### Team Growth

  - Engineering: 12 → 30 (build vertical-specific features)
