## BUFFER: $500k (10%)

- Higher-than-expected hiring costs or salaries (tight talent market)

- Acceleration of product roadmap (if sales traction exceeds forecast)
- Unforeseen market opportunities (strategic hiring, partnerships, acquisitions)
- Working capital (customer support infrastructure, tools, etc.)

**Allocation strategy:**
- 50% reserved for operational contingency (use if needed, Q1-Q4 2026)
- 30% reserved for growth acceleration (available Q3-Q4 2026 if traction strong)
- 20% reserved for strategic opportunities (partnerships, talent, tools)

## FINANCIAL IMPACT

### Burn Rate Improvement
- **Current burn**: $150k/month (before Series A)
- **Month 1 (Series A close)**: Burn = $150k - $50k ops revenue = -$100k
- **Month 6**: Burn = $150k - $200k ops revenue = -$50k (decreased burn)
- **Month 12 (Q4 2026)**: Burn = $200k - $200k = $0 (break-even)
- **Month 18 (Q2 2027)**: Burn = $150k - $300k = +$150k profit (positive cash flow)

### Cash Runway Analysis
- **Starting cash**: $5M Series A
- **Month 0-6**: Burn $600k, end with $4.4M
- **Month 6-12**: Burn $600k, end with $3.8M (break-even by month 12 = last $0.5M for Q1 buffer)
- **Month 12+**: Positive cash flow, no additional funding needed until Series B (Q3 2027)

**Key insight:** $5M is sufficient runway to profitability with 10-12 month timeline
